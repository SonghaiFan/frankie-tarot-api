# PKT 1911 meaning audit

All 78 identities and both orientations were checked against the supplied English source. Primary meanings now contain that source text and editorial Chinese summaries; the prior project-curated meanings remain separately available as modernMeanings. Art assets, IDs and card names are unchanged.

Source SHA-256: `dd7e239c0e6dbe313550279359bf0ffc9d61c3e2d2389eeb706d409088ef2b69`

## Important findings

- Ten of Swords: prior project fields placed the PKT reversed advantage/success passage under upright. Corrected for new readings.
- Two of Cups: III.2 does not supply a reversed clause. Its reversed “Passion” comes from III.4 and has a separate citation; it is not an invented separation meaning.
- The Fool, Death and The Star illustrate material differences between historical divinatory lists, pictorial symbolism and modern interpretations. Those differences are retained rather than claimed to be literal equivalents.
- Other contradictory or conditional readings in Waite are preserved in the English source. Chinese text is explicitly an editorial summary, not a claimed original translation.
- Historical references to complexion, gender, illness, death and legal outcomes are preserved as historical evidence and contextualized in the API-owned prompt, not used for identity classification or factual predictions.

## Spread compatibility

CELTIC crown/foundation/passing/approaching positions and labels are synchronized with III.7 using a fixed left-facing convention. The ten-card app version is an explicit adaptation without automatic significator selection or redraw. Other spreads remain modern extensions. API legacyDefinition preserves the previous spread semantics and full prompt for pre-versioned readings. New readings should freeze the returned spread definition and card meanings in their private recovery state.

## Rollout compatibility

REST and MCP spread reads/draws default to `revision=legacy` for older clients. New clients explicitly request `revision=pkt-1911-v1`. Both carry API-owned templates and an immutable compatibility definition; Frankie requests the new revision and freezes each reading's selected semantics. The card catalog exposes PKT primary meanings plus the previous modern meanings separately.
